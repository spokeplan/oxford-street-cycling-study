# Figures: press kit

Map figures for the article on a low-stress cycling route between Marble Arch and Tottenham Court Road after the Oxford Street cycling ban. Each image carries its title, legend, the OpenStreetMap extract date and the credit line. Every number printed on a figure is read directly from `results/` at render time.

**Which file to use:**
- **Print / press / slides:** `<name>.png` or `<name>.webp` (3,200 px wide)
- **Web / blog embeds:** `<name>-1600.webp` (1,600 px wide)
- **Social / link preview card:** [`fix-hero-og.jpg`](fix-hero-og.jpg) (1,200 × 630 px)

**Licence and credit:** Figures are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please credit with the line printed on each image:

> © OpenStreetMap contributors · basemap © OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles · Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0 · Powered by TfL Open Data · spokeplan.com

All figures show the study's best-evidence grading ([docs/lts.md](../docs/lts.md#best-evidence-run)): counts first (DfT and published counts), then TfL's modelled flows (LAEI 2022), and guesses only for streets with neither ("streets with no traffic figure"). The main reading does not count those unmeasured streets as calm; the quiet reading treats them as quiet.

## Where the numbers come from

- The grading drawn (`stress-map-readings`) and used to rebuild the routes the sets open (`fix-hero`, `fix-minimum`): the best-evidence classified ways, built in memory by `bestEvidenceWays` (`scripts/lts/sensitivity.ts`), tables `furth2017-low` (quiet) and `furth2017-high` (busy), scenario `today`.
- `fix-hero`, `fix-minimum`: the length to convert is `union_converted_m` in `results/lts/sensitivity/side-comparison.csv`, grading `best-evidence`, sides `north-only` and `south-only`; it must equal the union row of the side's own `results/lts/sensitivity/side-comparison/best-evidence/<table>/<side>/gap-closing-contraflow.csv`. The streets come from that run's `gap-closing-contraflow.geojson` (solution `min-union`). The route each set opens is rebuilt on the side's own network (`openedRoutes`, `scripts/lts/best-evidence.ts`) and must match the comparison's `ab_route_m` and `ba_route_m`. Legends, callouts and panel labels print lengths in km to two significant figures ("about 1.3 km"). The `fix-hero` title prints the range of the two lengths drawn rounded to whole km, or to one significant figure below 1 km ("about 2 km" when both round alike, otherwise "1 to 2 km"). The lead image and the card draw the busy reading (table `furth2017-high`); the lead image's note gives the quiet reading's lengths (`furth2017-low`). `fix-hero` labels 11 anchor streets (including Portman Square, Cavendish Square and Park Lane's crossing); `fix-minimum` labels every converted link.
- `fix-frequency`: the 32 best-evidence runs, `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/{furth2017-low,furth2017-high}/*/gap-closing*.geojson` (solution `min-union`), one per row of `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/runs.csv`; a street counts in a run when a stretch of it is converted. The counts must agree with `sensitivity_runs` in `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/frequency.csv`.
- `stress-map`: the generous grading, the best-evidence robustness check `guesses-zero` (`scripts/lts/robustness.ts`: every remaining class guess at no traffic), built in memory with `bestEvidenceWays` and `zeroDefaults` (`scripts/lts/sensitivity.ts`), table `furth2017-high`. Each reach area is every calm edge reachable from its trip end along calm arcs, one-way rules as the routing graph has them (`buildGraph`, `scripts/lts/route.ts`), from the node the trip end snaps to in the gap-closing search. A street's colour follows what its grade rests on: graded by the Furth table on a class default (`adt_source` class-default) is "no traffic figure". The title is checked against the data: the two areas share no node, not even with the streets stressful by their lanes alone made passable, and the ways drawn have no calm path, which must agree with `ab_connected_lowstress` and `ba_connected_lowstress` of the `guesses-zero` row in `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/robustness.csv`. The labels name major barrier streets inside the frame (Wigmore Street, Mortimer Street, Oxford Street, Park Lane, Regent Street, Brook Street and Great Marlborough Street).
- `stress-map-readings`: the best-evidence classified ways as above. The title is checked against the low-stress connectivity of the ways drawn, which must agree with the `ab_connected_lowstress` and `ba_connected_lowstress` columns of the best-evidence rows of `results/lts/sensitivity-summary.csv`. The rings are every count point whose figure grades a way by the Furth table (on its own way or carried along its road), located in `results/dft/countpoints-matched.csv` and `results/published-counts/counts-matched.csv`.
- `options`: the northern and southern lengths as for `fix-hero` (`furth2017-high`). The pedestrian-zone bar is `union_converted_m` of the row `furth2017-high`, `today`, `allow_oxford` true, `contraflow` true in `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/runs.csv`. Its split adds up `length_m` of that run's union features in `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/furth2017-high/today/gap-closing-allow-oxford-contraflow.geojson` whose `street` is Oxford Street (links and crossings), the rest being other streets. The Wigmore Street track option is not drawn: the results do not record the track's own length.
- `evidence`: the streets of the two `furth2017-high` side sets as for `fix-hero`, each way's `adt_source` from the best-evidence classified ways: `count` and `road` are counted, `laei` TfL's model, `class-default` no traffic figure. The title is checked: counted carries more than half the northern set, TfL's model more than half the southern.
- `long-trip`: the two routes in `inputs/spokeplan-routes/` (SpokePlan's Road and City presets, its own output, see that README); the key prints each file's `distance`. The streets named on the routes are those of the study's OSM ways within 15 m of the line (only where the extract covers it). The parks, Constitution Hill and Birdcage Walk are placed by hand (`LONG_TRIP_PLACES`, `scripts/figures/style.ts`): the two streets lie outside the study's ways, and their names were read from the full extract's ways along the City route. The underlay is the southern route's converted edges (`furth2017-high`, `south-only`, as for `fix-hero`) that the Road route runs along: within 8 m and 30° of the line. The title is checked: across Oxford Street's length the City route stays south of the Road route.
- `routes`: `results/lts/sensitivity/best-evidence/<table>/today/{A-B,B-A}-{confident,cautious}.geojson`.
- Extract date: `results/extract-info.json`.
